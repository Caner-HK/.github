## 🚀 What we're making

### DeskTile

**Small Tile. Big Style.**

What started with a railway passenger information display has grown into **DeskTile**: a compact, programmable device for your desk, with its own operating system and an open application platform.

There's a lot going on behind that little screen: a touch-first interface, system apps, an SDK, app permissions and sandboxing, and the `.dtapp` application format. **MTR PIDS** and **MTR Timeline** remain part of its identity — one brings railway arrival information to the desk; the other tells the story of Hong Kong's railways across history and into the future.

And the best part of an open platform? We don't have to think of every possible use ourselves.

### 🌦️ CWC Weather

**Your weather starts now.**

Is it about to rain? How will the weather change over the next few hours? What about tomorrow?

**CWC Weather (Caner Weather Channel)** puts the answers in a clearer picture, from current conditions and feels-like temperature to hourly and daily forecasts, precipitation trends, and detailed weather measurements. Keep an eye on multiple places, whether they're close to home or somewhere you're heading next.

We've also put a lot of care into the way weather *looks* and *reads*. Good information shouldn't be hard to understand.

[Website](https://weather.caner.hk) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=hk.caner.weather)

### ☁️ CWC CLens

**Read clouds in a snap.**

Ever looked up and wondered, *“What kind of cloud is that?”*

**CWC CLens** uses on-device AI to help you explore the sky. Take a photo or choose one from your gallery, and explore recognition results covering **10 fundamental cloud genera**, plus **contrails and clear sky**. Confidence scores, alternative candidates, and reliability hints help you understand what the model can — and can't — tell you.

But recognising a cloud is only the start. CLens also includes a cloud encyclopaedia, observation guides, comparisons between similar cloud types, sky knowledge, and quizzes. The goal isn't just to give you an answer; it's to make the next sky you see a little more interesting.

Core cloud recognition works on your device, without uploading the photo for inference or requiring an account for the core experience.

[Website](https://clens.caner.hk) | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=hk.caner.cwcclens) | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/cwc-clens/id6795224135)

### 🌡️ CEI — Comfort Environment Index

What's the difference between the temperature on a screen and how it actually *feels* outside?

**CEI** explores that question with a **0–100 environmental comfort index**, bringing together weather conditions, thermal perception, air quality, and context such as climate and season. Its core is open source and designed for use in different kinds of software and devices.

[GitHub repository](https://github.com/Caner-HK/CEI-Comfort-Environment-Index)
